# Code of conduct

This project welcomes respectful, constructive participation from people with different levels of experience.

Be considerate in issues, pull requests, and discussions. Focus criticism on ideas, code, and reproducible behavior rather than individuals. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.

To report a conduct concern privately, contact the maintainer using the contact information on their GitHub profile. Please do not disclose another person's private information in a public issue.
